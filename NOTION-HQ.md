## Problem & idea
**Problem:** Students interested in cyber security don't know which of the many cyber jobs fits them, and the free learning material (roadmaps, course paths) is overwhelming before you've picked a direction.

**Idea:** Career Quest. A short Vibe Check matches you to one of 12 cyber careers, then you learn it in Duolingo-style five-minute lessons (XP, streaks, hearts). Classes and societies can play the same questions as a live Kahoot-style battle on their phones. Progress and a study plan sync to Notion.

**Who it's for:** students thinking about cyber security, first-year cyber students, and societies or lecturers who want a quick live quiz.

## Research
- Looked at past hackathon winners in quiz/learning apps: generic "notes → quiz" apps win small prizes; the bigger wins went to learning apps aimed at one clear audience or subject. So we picked one subject, cyber careers.
- Question bank: 480 questions, 12 careers, 40 each, every one citing a source (OWASP Top 10 for LLMs 2025, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-53 / 800-30 / 800-115, NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATT&CK, CIS Controls).
- Next-step labs mapped from TryHackMe learning paths.
- Design studied from a hands-on Duolingo lesson walkthrough (layout, buttons, path, feedback).
