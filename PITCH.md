# Career Quest: pitch (≈4 min) + demo

Before going up: host screen open on `/host` → Demo round → **3 questions**, lobby showing the PIN and QR. Phone ready for the photo (hand it to a teammate).

## 0:00 Problem (30s)
"Cyber security has loads of different jobs: pen tester, SOC analyst, cloud security, malware analyst, and more. If you're a student, you don't know which one fits you, and the free material is a wall of roadmaps and courses. Most people give up before they start."

> If you ran the lunch poll, say the real number here: "We asked __ people at lunch. __ of them didn't know what a ___ actually does." **Only say numbers you actually collected.**

## 0:30 What we built (30s)
"Career Quest. A short Vibe Check matches you to one of 12 cyber careers. Then you learn it the way Duolingo teaches a language: five-minute lessons, XP, streaks, hearts. And when a class or a society wants to play together, it turns into a live Kahoot-style battle."

## 1:00 Live demo (≈90s): the room plays
"Get your phones out. Scan the QR, pick a nickname."
- Start the 3-question demo round. Let it run. Don't talk over the timer.
- After the podium: "That last question was a prompt injection. That's a real attack on AI apps, and you just learned it in 20 seconds."

## 2:30 Lesson path + Byte (30s)
Switch to the lesson path. Answer one question right, one wrong, so Byte reacts.
"This is Byte, our mascot. We built and animated him ourselves."

## 3:00 Why it's real (40s)
- "480 questions across 12 careers, and every single one cites its source: OWASP, NIST, MITRE ATT&CK, CIS Controls. None of it is made-up AI content."
- "Each career links to real hands-on labs on TryHackMe as the next step."
- Notion: "Hit 'Send my progress to Notion' and your study plan lands in Notion: your three weakest lessons and the next lab. A society can point everyone at one shared Notion database and get a live leaderboard." (Show it only if Prompt 4 works. If not, say it's next.)

## 3:40 Close (20s)
"I'm a first-year cyber security student at TU Dublin. On Monday our team came 5th at the Break the Agent CTF. We built the thing we wish we'd had on day one. Thanks."

## Likely judge questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How is this different from Duolingo / Kahoot / TryHackMe? | Duolingo and Kahoot don't do careers. TryHackMe assumes you already know which path you want. We cover the step before: which job, and the basics of it. |
| Where do the questions come from? | Hand-checked bank, 480 questions, each with a cited source (OWASP, NIST CSF / SP 800-53 / AI RMF, MITRE ATT&CK, CIS Controls). |
| Did you use AI? | Yes: Lovable built the app from our prompts, and AI helped draft the question bank, which we then checked against the cited sources. (Say what's true for your team.) |
| What did you prepare beforehand? | Idea, mascot design, prompts and the question bank. The app itself was built today. |
| What's next? | Lecturers running battles in class with their own module questions; societies using the shared Notion leaderboard. |
| How do you stop cheating in the battle? | The server clock decides timing and rejects late answers; options are shuffled per question. |
